# Business Analytics — Entity Schemas (SCHEMAS ONLY)

**Status: SCHEMAS ONLY. No business results, dashboards, or computed
metrics exist yet.** This document describes the data model that a
future "Business Hub" analytics layer would read from — the
underlying tables were added as empty DDL in
`05_SQL/schema/dayjoy_full_production_schema.sql` (see the
`-- SCHEMA ONLY, NO PRODUCTION DATA EXISTS YET` comments on each
table). Nothing here should be read as an actual reported number,
target, or achievement. This is a data-model / capability spec, not
a report.

Source of truth for column-level detail: the CREATE TABLE statements
in `05_SQL/schema/dayjoy_full_production_schema.sql`. This document
gives the analytics-facing summary and lists what could be computed
once real data is populated.

---

## 1. Entities

### orders / order_items
Transactional e-commerce records: one `orders` row per checkout, with
`order_items` line items referencing `products`. Carries status
(`pending`→`delivered`/`cancelled`/`returned`), payment status, and
totals for amount, BV, and PV.

### sales
MLM sales-recognition records used for BV/PV/commission calculation.
Distinct from `orders` — can exist without an order (offline/manual
entry) via a nullable `order_id`. Tagged `retail`, `self_use`, or
`repeat_order`.

### customers
End buyers, optionally linked to a `profiles` auth record and to the
`distributor` who referred them (`referred_by_distributor_id`).

### distributors
MLM participants. Self-referencing `sponsor_distributor_id` encodes
the binary upline; `current_rank_id` links to the existing
`plan_ranks` table; `placement_leg` records left/right binary
placement.

### leaders
A 1:1 specialization of `distributors` for those who have been
promoted past a leadership threshold; linked to a `team`.

### team_members (`teams` + `leaders` + `distributors`)
`teams` groups distributors under a `lead_distributor_id`; individual
membership is inferred through `distributors`/`leaders` records that
reference a team, since no separate `team_members` junction table
was in the requested scope. If a formal many-to-many membership model
is needed later (a distributor active on multiple teams), add a
`team_members` junction table.

### bv_transactions / pv_transactions
Ledger-style records of Business Volume and Personal Volume
generated by `sales`, scoped to a distributor and a period
(`period_week` for BV to match the existing weekly matching cadence
in `business_matching_rules`; `period_month` for PV).

### targets
Distributor-level goals (`sales`, `recruitment`, `rank_promotion`,
`bv`, `pv`) with a period window, target value, and running
`achieved_value`, plus a derived `status`.

### rank_history
Append-only log of rank achievements per distributor, linked to
`plan_ranks`. `is_current = true` marks the distributor's present
rank (enforced by a partial unique index).

### support_tickets
Customer/distributor support cases with category, priority, status,
assignment, and SLA due timestamp.

## 2. What the future Business Hub could calculate

All of the below are **capabilities the schema supports**, not
figures that exist today.

### Sales & revenue
- Daily / weekly / monthly / yearly sales totals — `sum(sales.total_amount)` or `sum(orders.total_amount)` grouped by `date_trunc()` on `sale_date` / `order_date`.
- Sales by product / category / brand — join `sales` → `products` → `categories`/`brands`.
- Repeat-order rate — `sales.sale_type = 'repeat_order'` share over time.
- Average order value — `avg(orders.total_amount)`.

### Team & distributor performance
- Team-level rollups — `sales`/`orders` joined through `distributors.team_id`-equivalent path (via `leaders.team_id` or a future `team_members` table).
- Leaderboards by BV, PV, or order count per distributor per period.
- New distributor activations vs. sponsor — using `distributors.sponsor_distributor_id` and `joining_date`.

### BV / PV
- Weekly BV totals per distributor / per leg (`bv_transactions.leg`) — feeds the existing `business_matching_rules` weekly-closing logic.
- Monthly PV totals per distributor — feeds personal-qualification checks.
- BV/PV trend over time (week-over-week, month-over-month deltas).

### Targets & rank progress
- Target attainment rate — `achieved_value / target_value` per `targets` row, rolled up by `period_type`.
- Rank-progression funnel — count of distributors per current rank (`distributors.current_rank_id`), and time-to-promotion using `rank_history.achieved_date` deltas.
- Rank downgrade/plateau detection — comparing successive `rank_history` rows per distributor.

### Inactivity, growth & alerts
- Inactive distributor detection — no `sales`/`bv_transactions` rows within a rolling window (e.g. last N weeks) despite `distributors.status = 'active'`.
- Growth rate — new `customers`/`distributors` per period vs. prior period.
- Churn signals — `orders.order_status = 'cancelled'/'returned'` rate; `support_tickets` volume/category spikes.
- SLA breach alerts — `support_tickets` where `now() > sla_due_at` and `status` not in (`resolved`,`closed`).
- Target-miss alerts — `targets.status = 'missed'` or trending toward miss before `period_end`.
