# Dayjoy Marketing – Brochure Data Extract (July 2026)

## Files in this package
1. Dayjoy_Company_Profile.csv - Company background, founders, manufacturing, certifications, awards
2. Dayjoy_Products_Catalog.csv - 86 products across all sub-brands with benefits, ingredients, usage, pricing
3. Dayjoy_Health_Recommendation_Chart.csv - Health condition to product mapping

## Sub-brands covered
Asthprash, Curind (Health Care), Wild Muse (Personal Care), Amara Beauty (Skincare),
Aayushmaan (Spices), YumDum (Ready-to-Eat), HomeShine (Home Care), Domatti (Clothing),
HappyGrow (Agriculture), Aqua Essentials

## Notes
- All prices are MRP in INR as listed in the July 2026 brochure.
- Data extracted and organized from the attached PDF brochure only.
