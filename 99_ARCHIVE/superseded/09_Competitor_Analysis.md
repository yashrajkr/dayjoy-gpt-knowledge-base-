## 3. Company Profiles

### 3.1 Amway India

**Company Overview**

**VERIFIED:** Amway India is a wholly owned subsidiary of Alticor Global Holdings Inc. (USA), operating since 1995 and positioned as a leading direct selling company in India’s health and wellness category. [web:105][web:106][web:109][web:110][web:111]

**Product Categories**

**VERIFIED:** Amway India offers **130–140+ products** across categories such as: Nutrition & Wellness (Nutrilite), Beauty (Artistry), Personal Care, Home Care, and Consumer Durables. [web:106][web:109][web:110][web:111]

**Target Audience**

**VERIFIED:** Middle-income and aspirational urban consumers seeking science-backed nutrition, beauty, and home products; individuals interested in entrepreneurial income via direct selling. [web:105][web:110][web:111]

**Business Model & Distribution**

**VERIFIED:**

- Multi-level marketing (MLM) direct selling model. [web:106][web:109][web:110][web:111]

- 3+ million IBOs globally, 550,000+ distributors in India. [web:106][web:109]
- Direct selling via personal recommendations, home parties, and online ordering, with limited physical retail. [web:110][web:111]

**Market Positioning**

**VERIFIED:** Premium science-backed wellness and daily-use FMCG brand with strong trust on Nutrilite and global brand equity; positioned as a compliant and reputable direct selling leader. [web:105][web:110][web:111]

**Brand Strengths**

- Large, mature distributor base and training ecosystem. [web:106][web:109]
- Science-backed products, strong R&D narrative (Nutrilite). [web:105]
- Money-back guarantees and compliance advocacy. [web:109]
- Omnichannel capabilities (online ordering, mobile apps, limited retail). [web:110][web:111]

**Weaknesses**
