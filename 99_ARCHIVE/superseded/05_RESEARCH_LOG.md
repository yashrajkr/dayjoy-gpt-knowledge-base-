## 4. Key Findings

### Company
- Dayjoy Marketing Private Limited is a private limited company incorporated in 2018 with an office in Kota, Rajasthan. [01_Company_Research.md][web:5][web:12]
- Dayjoy operates as a direct selling wellness brand. [02_Business_Model.md][web:16][web:68]

### Products
- Dayjoy publicly exposes product pages for key SKUs in health care, personal care, and agriculture/veterinary. [03_Product_Research.md][web:37][web:39][web:50][web:25]
- Product data is incomplete for many SKUs and requires more master data. [12_Research_Gap_Analysis.md]

### Distributor System
- Distributor registration is free, but governed by strict eligibility and compliance rules. [04_Distributor_System.md][web:69]
- Compensation plan includes multiple incentives, bonuses, loyalty, and rewards. [04_Distributor_System.md][web:68]

### Policies
- Policies are publicly documented for shipping, returns, refunds, cancellation, privacy, and terms. [05_Policies.md][web:59][web:4][web:69][web:73]

### Customer Journey
- Customer and distributor journeys span awareness, research, order, support, repeat purchase, and loyalty. [07_Customer_Journey.md]

### Business Processes
- Core workflows include product inquiry, ordering, payment, shipping, return/refund, complaints, training, and approvals. [08_Business_Processes.md]

### Competitors
- Amway India, Oriflame India, and Himalaya Wellness are key market references. [09_Competitor_Analysis.md]

### Pain Points
- Major pain points center on product selection, support delays, distributor complexity, and knowledge fragmentation. [10_Pain_Points.md]

### AI Opportunities
- AI should prioritize knowledge base, support automation, distributor tools, recommendation engines, and analytics. [11_AI_Opportunities.md]

### Research Gaps
- Technology stack, CRM/ERP, inventory, APIs, and detailed portal capabilities remain unknown. [12_Research_Gap_Analysis.md]

## 10. Lessons Learned

### Successful approaches
- Structured mission-based research worked well.
- Separating known facts from gaps improved clarity.
- Using official sources first preserved trust.

### Common challenges
- Public pages often provide partial information.
- Product and system details are not fully exposed publicly.
- Some public references contain duplicated or aliased SKUs.

### Research limitations
- No direct access to internal systems.
- No internal product master or portal screenshots.
- No live API documentation.

### Recommendations for future research
- Hold client workshops by department.
- Capture system inventories early.
- Maintain a change log and fact/gap discipline.
