# Income Claim Policy

**Sources:** `01_CANONICAL/policies/Income_Disclaimer_Dayjoy.pdf` (primary), `08_GOVERNANCE/answer_policy/ABSTAIN_AND_LABELING_POLICY.md` (Category 5: Business/income claims), `07_ANALYTICS/metrics/dayjoy_income_opportunities.csv`.

## Source of truth
`Income_Disclaimer_Dayjoy.pdf` states, verbatim: earnings/income statements "are estimates only and are not guaranteed," individual results vary, examples "are not to be interpreted as guarantees, promises, representations, or assurances of income levels," Dayjoy "does not present our business as a 'get-rich-quick' scheme," and success "depends on various factors, including your individual capacity, business experience, expertise, and level of commitment." Testimonials "highlight exceptional outcomes and may not apply to the average participant." Past earnings are "not indicative of future results."

## Rule
- Never state or imply that a specific person will earn a specific amount.
- Any worked earning example from the GrowthX compensation deck may be cited **only as illustrative**, with the source page, and must always be paired with the Income Disclaimer language above.
- `07_ANALYTICS/metrics/dayjoy_income_opportunities.csv` is tier **INFERRED** (corpus mention-frequency signal, not real earnings data). Never present it as realized/actual income data.
- Always cite `Income_Disclaimer_Dayjoy.pdf` alongside any income-adjacent answer.

## Disclaimer (mandatory)
> "Earnings/income statements are estimates only and not guaranteed. Individual results vary and depend on your own effort, experience, and commitment — this is not a guarantee of income." (Income_Disclaimer_Dayjoy.pdf)

## Abstention rule
Abstain if the user asks "how much will I earn" or requests a guaranteed/personalized income figure. Do not compute or project an individual's earnings from the compensation plan tables — restate that income results vary and are not guaranteed, and offer the illustrative worked example only if explicitly requested and clearly labeled as hypothetical.
